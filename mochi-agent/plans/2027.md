# Plan 2027: nurseisangel

**Status: proposed 2026-11-18 (t1-worker2), for t1-manager, then the Owner.** It is short on purpose: the site exists for
campaign 001 First Cent, and the year's direction depends on what the first season (Dec 2026) shows.

## Direction
One landing page for one market (D: nurses and their gift buyers) that sends visitors to our own products (the KDP
planner, Merch tees, Printify posters), counts every step, and is promoted through the Owner's Nurse Is Angel Facebook
page. No ads until the funnel design's rules allow a test (R74-A).

## The year's goals (targets per quarter)
| Goal | Q1 | Q2 | Q3 | Q4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Real sales attributed to the site (all products) | 5 | 10 | 10 | 30 (gift season) |
| Store visits through the site's links (`go_clicks`) | 150 | 200 | 200 | 600 |
| Products live on the page | 12 (planner, 5 tees, 5 posters + the 2028 planner draft) | +3 (Nurses Week, May 6–12) | +2 | +5 (gift season) |

## Calendar
- **Q1:** the 2027 planner sells through January; Valentine's for nurses (a tee + a poster); read the December season against METRICS.md.
- **Q2:** **Nurses Week (May 6–12)** is the year's second peak: a collection refresh and a launch post.
- **Q3:** keep the page fresh; the 2028 planner built by September (KDP lead time).
- **Q4:** the gift season again, from the December 2026 numbers: what sold, at which price (the poll), from which source.

## Not in this plan
No new paid tools; no ad spend without the R74-A test plan and the Owner's yes; no medical claims; trademarks checked
for every phrase (as #190).
