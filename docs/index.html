For the ones who stay through the night

Nurses hold hands on the hardest days, remember names, and keep watch while everyone else sleeps. Most of the time, no one says thank you.

Nurse Is Angel is a small collection of gifts that say it for you: tees a nurse will actually wear on a day off, and quiet prints for a home or a break room.

Who it's for

For the nurse in your life: your mom, your partner, your friend from school, the coworker who covered your shift. And for the nurse who cared for someone you love.

Each design is one honest line, set in clear type. No slogans about being a superhero. Just thank you.
