Privacy

Last updated: 18 November 2026.

nurseisangel.com is run by PTK Studio. The products are designed by Honey Storm, our product line. The site has no accounts, no sign-up and no advertising.

What we count

When you open a page here, or tap a product link, the page sends a short note to our counter at api.88eggs.com (PTK Studio's 88eggs service). The note says which of our pages you're on, which product you tapped or opened, the campaign tag in the link you came from, and the time. The campaign tag is one word from a fixed list (for example ig for Instagram, pin for Pinterest); anything else is saved as "other". If a product shows the question "What would you pay for this?" and you tap an answer, the note also says which price range you picked. Nothing else is kept with it, and it isn't linked to you, so if you answer again on another visit, it counts again.

That's all the counter keeps. It doesn't store your IP address, a cookie, your browser or device details, or the address of the page you came from. To stop floods of fake visits, it keeps a scrambled code made from your IP address (a keyed hash that changes every day) for 10 minutes, then forgets it. Visits from search-engine robots are dropped, and test visits we make ourselves are marked and left out of our numbers.

We use the counts only to see which products people are interested in. We delete them after 365 days.

Visit statistics (Google Analytics)

We also use Google Analytics (part of Google's Firebase) to understand how the site is used: which pages are viewed, how far down a page you scroll, which product or outside link you tapped, how you arrived (for example from Instagram), your device and browser type, and roughly where you are (country and city, worked out from your IP address). Google processes your IP address to do this; Google Analytics doesn't store it.

Outside the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google Analytics sets cookies on this site (named _ga and _ga_…) to tell a returning visit from a new one; they expire after up to 2 years. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, no Analytics cookies are set: the page sends Google only cookieless signals, which Google uses to estimate visit numbers.

It's never used for advertising: advertising cookies, ad personalization and Google signals are switched off, and we don't share the data with advertisers. Google Analytics keeps the detailed data for 2 months. You can read how Google uses this data in How Google uses information from sites that use its services and Google's privacy policy. To opt out, use the Google Analytics opt-out add-on, or block cookies for this site in your browser.

Cookies

This site's own code sets no cookies and uses no browser storage. The only cookies are Google Analytics', outside the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, as described above. There are no ads or advertising trackers.

Product links

Product links open a short page on nurseisangel.com/go/… that sends you straight on to the store (Amazon or shop.88eggs.com). The link may carry a tag naming this site. The store's own privacy policy applies there.

Feedback

If you send us feedback in Nurse Is Angel (through the feedback button, form or app screen), we receive your message, the kind of feedback you chose, an optional star rating, the page or app version you were using, and your language setting. We ask for your email only if you want a reply, and we keep it only if you tick the box allowing us to contact you; we delete it after at most 90 days. We don't use cookies or tracking for feedback, and we don't collect device identifiers. We also store the IP address your feedback was sent from, to prevent abuse; it is deleted with your feedback after one year. Feedback is stored by PTK Studio's 88eggs service (hosted in the United States), used only to fix problems and improve Nurse Is Angel, never sold or shared, and deleted after one year. To have your feedback deleted sooner, contact us at hello@ptk-studio.com.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As our host, GitHub processes your IP address to deliver the pages to you, under GitHub's privacy statement. Our counter and the feedback form at api.88eggs.com run behind Cloudflare, which processes your IP address to pass those requests to us, under Cloudflare's privacy policy.
